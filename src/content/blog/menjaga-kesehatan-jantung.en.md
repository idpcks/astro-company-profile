Heart disease remains the leading cause of death in Indonesia. You can
change most of its risk factors through everyday habits.

## Five habits you can start today

### 1. Move for 30 minutes every day

It doesn't have to be the gym. Brisk walking, taking the stairs, or
gardening are enough to train your heart. Aim for 150 minutes per week.

### 2. Cut back on salt and fried food

The ideal salt limit is one teaspoon per day. Fried snacks are tempting,
but reused oil and trans fats are the main enemies of your blood vessels.

### 3. Sleep 7–8 hours

Chronic sleep deprivation raises blood pressure and appetite. Your heart
"works overtime" whenever you stay up late.

### 4. Quit smoking

Twenty minutes after quitting, blood pressure begins to normalize. One
year later, the risk of coronary heart disease has dropped by half.

### 5. Manage stress

Unmanaged stress triggers high blood pressure. Exercise, worship, hobbies,
and talking with loved ones are "medicine" most of us underestimate.

## Cardiac screening: when is it needed?

If you experience chest pain during activity, unusual shortness of breath,
or a family history of heart disease, don't delay consulting a
cardiologist. Our Heart Clinic is equipped with EKG and echocardiography
for a thorough examination.
