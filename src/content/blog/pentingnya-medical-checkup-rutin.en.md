"Do I really need a checkup? I feel fine." This is the question we hear
most often when educating patients about periodic health screenings.

## Feeling healthy is not the same as being healthy

Hypertension, high cholesterol, and early diabetes are nearly symptomless.
All three are the leading risk factors for heart disease and stroke in
Indonesia — and all three can be detected with simple tests.

## What gets examined?

A basic medical checkup package usually covers:

1. Blood pressure and body mass index.
2. Blood type, complete blood count, blood sugar, and lipid profile.
3. Kidney and liver function.
4. Routine urine tests.

For those over 40, an EKG, chest X-ray, and lifestyle consultation are
typically added.

## How often?

The general recommendation is once a year. However, if you have risk
factors such as family history, smoking, or obesity, your doctor may
advise shorter intervals.
